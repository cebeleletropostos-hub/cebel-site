About

The Brazilian Clean Energy Company.

We're an electric mobility company from Rio de Janeiro state. We build a network of EV chargers and clean energy generation, bringing charging to the places where people already spend their day.

Our story

Charging where drivers already are.

CEBEL was founded in 2026 in Rio de Janeiro from a simple observation: the electric fleet is growing faster than the charging network, especially outside the capital. EV drivers in Itaguaí, the Lakes Region or the Baixada still drive around wondering where they'll charge.

Our first station was installed at Smart Fit Itaguaí. Then came Rio das Ostras, and today the network is expanding to Irajá, Nilópolis, Araruama and Saquarema.

We work on a concession model: CEBEL invests in the equipment, operates and maintains the station, and the host receives a share of the revenue. That's how chargers reach businesses that would never invest in one on their own.
