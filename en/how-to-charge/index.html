For drivers

Charging is simple.

All from your phone. Park, plug in, start the charge in the app and pay only for the energy you used.

See stations {% include icons.html i="arrow" %}

Step by step

From parking spot to full battery

  1. Download the app

    Our stations run on the EzVolt app for Android and iPhone. Create an account and add credit, from R$ 20, by Pix or card.

  2. Park and plug in

    Park in the marked CEBEL spot and plug the connector into your car.

  3. Start charging

    In the app, choose the station or scan the charger's QR code and start the session.

  4. Track and finish

    See the energy delivered in real time. When you're done, stop it in the app and the amount comes out of your credit.

Charger types

Fast or slow?

{% include icons.html i="bolt" %}DC · 40 kW

Fast charging

30 minutes to 1 hour for a good part of the battery. Ideal if you're passing through.

{% include icons.html i="car" %}AC · 7 kW

Slow charging

A few hours to fill up. Ideal when you stay a while, like at the gym or at work.

{% include icons.html i="shield" %}Connectors

Brazilian standard

Type 2 for slow charging and CCS2 for fast charging, the standards on most cars sold in Brazil.

FAQ

Driver questions

Do I need to be a Smart Fit member to charge?

No. The stations are open to any driver during the parking lot's opening hours.

Is my car compatible?

Electric and plug-in hybrid cars sold in Brazil use Type 2 (slow charging) and CCS2 (fast charging). Check each station's type on the stations page.

What if the charger doesn't work?

Message us on WhatsApp at {{ site.whatsapp_display }} and tell us which station. In most cases our team can restart the charger remotely.

Can I leave my car in the spot after charging?

We don't charge idle fees, but please free up the spot when you're done so other drivers can charge too.
