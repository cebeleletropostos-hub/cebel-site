How it works

From first contact to first charge

  1. Site visit

    We assess the parking spaces, the electrical panel and the site's traffic. Free of charge.

  2. Proposal and contract

    We define the number of chargers, spaces and the revenue share. A simple space-use agreement.

  3. Installation

    CEBEL buys the equipment and handles the electrical work and the utility approval.

  4. Operation

    Your site goes live in the app and on Google Maps. You receive your share every month, with a usage report.

FAQ

Questions from owners considering it

Do I pay anything?

No. Equipment, installation, the charger's electricity, maintenance and software are all covered by CEBEL.

How do I receive my share?

Monthly, with a report of the charges at your site. The standard share is {{ site.repasse_parceiro }}% of net revenue and can be adjusted depending on the site.

Do I need to reserve spaces for EVs only?

We recommend one or two marked spaces next to the charger. We decide the number with you during the site visit.

Can my electrical system handle it?

That's what the site visit confirms. When available capacity is low, there are solutions such as AC chargers or backup batteries.

How long is the contract?

Contracts usually run for a few years to cover the investment in equipment. Terms are discussed case by case.
