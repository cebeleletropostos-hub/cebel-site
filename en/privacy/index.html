Privacy

Privacy policy

How we handle the information you send through the website, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

Who we are

{{ site.razao_social }} ("CEBEL"), an electric vehicle charging company in Rio de Janeiro state. Contact: WhatsApp {{ site.whatsapp_display }}.

What we collect

When you fill in a form, we collect what you type: name, WhatsApp number, business name and type, city, number of parking spaces and your message. We also record where the visit came from (for example, an Instagram ad or a Google search) and the page where the form was sent.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to you, prepare partnership proposals and understand which channels bring contacts to the site. We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Measurement cookies

If you accept in the cookie notice, the site loads measurement tools from Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) to count visits and contacts coming from our ads. If you decline, these tools are not loaded. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser.

How long we keep it

We keep contact details while a conversation or partnership is ongoing, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data via WhatsApp {{ site.whatsapp_display }}.
